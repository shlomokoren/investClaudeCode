How to use Invest Dashboard

This app helps you screen a stock's financial health, then decide whether it looks like a good time to buy or sell based on its price trend.

1

Check the company's financials

Open the Financials tab and look up a stock you're curious about. It shows the income statement and cash flow statement — revenue, profit, and free cash flow trends — so you can judge whether the underlying business looks healthy.

2

If you like the financials, check the price trend

Switch to the Price Chart (150-day SMA) tab and add the ticker to your watch list. Each chart plots the stock's price against its 150-day simple moving average (SMA150) — a smoothed line of the average closing price over the last 150 trading days.

The simple decision rule

  • Price above SMA150 — the stock is in an uptrend; this is treated as a signal to buy.
  • Price below SMA150 — the stock is in a downtrend; this is treated as a signal to sell.

I picked up this price-vs-SMA150 rule from the Micha Stock YouTube podcast.

Not financial advice. This is one simple rule of thumb, not investment advice — I built this app purely to practice software development, not to guide real trading decisions. Do your own research (or talk to a licensed advisor) before buying or selling anything.
