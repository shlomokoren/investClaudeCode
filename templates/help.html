How to use Invest Dashboard

Invest Dashboard helps you research a stock — is the business healthy, is the price trending up or down — and keep an eye on the ones you follow. Sign in with Google and you get your own private watch list, shared across every tab. Add or remove tickers on the Price Chart tab; the change applies everywhere.

The tabs

Price Chart (150-day SMA)

What it is: each stock's price plotted against its 150-day moving average (SMA150), with a volume bar.

What it's for: seeing whether a stock is trending up or down right now.

How to use it: add tickers with Add symbol, click a chip to show or hide its chart, and pick a date range. Price above the SMA150 line is an uptrend; below it is a downtrend.

Financials

What it is: revenue, profit, free cash flow and EPS for one company, charted quarter by quarter or year by year.

What it's for: checking the business is healthy before you worry about the share price.

How to use it: type a ticker or click a chip, then switch between Quarterly and Annual. Look for steady growth rather than one good quarter.

Portfolio

What it is: your watch list with live price, today's change, the value of any shares you hold, and an allocation pie chart.

What it's for: seeing what you own and how it's doing today.

How to use it: enter a share count in the Position column — the totals and pie update instantly. Use the chips to filter and click a column header to sort.

Events

What it is: the next earnings date and the dividend schedule (ex-date, pay date, amount, yield) for each watch-list stock.

What it's for: knowing when a holding reports results or pays a dividend.

How to use it: it opens sorted by soonest event; click any header to re-sort, use the chips to filter. An est. tag means the earnings date is still an estimate.

News

What it is: recent Yahoo Finance headlines for the symbols you pick.

What it's for: a quick catch-up on what's moving your stocks.

How to use it: toggle symbols with the chips, choose how far back to look (2 days by default), and scroll the feed. Your selection is remembered next time.

A suggested workflow

  1. Check a company on Financials — is it growing?
  2. If it looks healthy, open the Price Chart — is it above or below its SMA150?
  3. Add it to your list and follow it from Portfolio, Events, and News.

The simple decision rule

  • Price above SMA150 — uptrend; treated as a buy signal.
  • Price below SMA150 — downtrend; treated as a sell signal.

This price-vs-SMA150 rule comes from the Micha Stock YouTube podcast.

Not financial advice. This is one rule of thumb, not investment advice — the app was built to practise software development, not to guide real trades. Do your own research (or talk to a licensed advisor) before buying or selling anything.
